## Reese's observations

> "Bring it back, and I'll pay you twenty more from my own purse" makes it ambiguous whether the party is getting 120+20 or just 20. "I'll pay you 20 from my own purse" would be better

> the red line and text on the map don't quite line up, and it's hard to read the text as a result

> same with the yellow "safe" text

> the records room is described as 8 sided, but i see 6 sides (unless it means the ceiling and floor are 2 sides)

> the triggers to tell players the contents of the notes and calendar entry are a bit inconsistently formatted. why is the note text in a read aloud box and the calendar text normal? i can infer what to reveal to the players fairly easily, but the formatting makes it take more thought than necessary

> the text "The desk and papers are small props, not barriers" seems unnecessary

> "Its four numbered wheels are marked “DAY / MONTH.” seems a bit too obvious

> a bit confused why the order is in a safe but the money is elsewhere. wouldn't the money be locked up and the order found more readily?

> "Nell warned Iona about a trap when she tried the door; Iona won't risk entering.", what if the party disables the trap and then tells iona about it? why does nell tell iona about the trap like it's a threat, but tell the party not to step on the string?

> finished the adventure. fine if the issues above are fixed. would run as is with several modifications that i'd have to do myself, but would likely prefer to ask a model to fix the issues
