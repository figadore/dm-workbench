## Author-side smoke checks

These are assistant inspection and deterministic document checks, **not human acceptance, review
minutes, editing-time evidence, or a playtest**. No scores or favorable human reactions were inferred.

- **Premise:** the revised text distinguishes today's open pay chest at the dock office from the old
  counting house's locked archive of employment records. Nell holds the payroll as settlement leverage;
  her initial warning demands that pursuers keep out, not just slow down. She may invite mediators to
  approach afterward. Iona takes the unspecified trap seriously but enters once shown a safe route.
  These causal repairs respond to human feedback, not a claim that earlier author checks proved them.
- **Information:** the date is in Entry Hall; the mnemonic is in Records; the safe and definite answer
  are in Strongroom. Neither clue requires opening the safe; the safe itself no longer spells out
  DAY / MONTH. Bringing Iona to inspect the archive offers another way to obtain the combination.
  Note and calendar entries share one inspection trigger and quotation format. Nell's claim, the
  clerk's reminder and the genuine signed order are distinguished. Iona's opening is attributed speech.
- **Map:** manually traced 1–2, 1–3, 3–4, 4–2, 4–5 and 5–2. Room names match the overlays. The net cord
  crosses the southern opening of room 2, not a newly invented corridor; the safe is in room 4's
  northern aisle. Five keyed areas group the open aisles and connecting passages into usable keys.
  The sole outside exit is Entry Hall's stairs; alternate internal routes are not secret escapes.
- **Important limitation:** that room graph has **two independent loops**, beyond the current Tier A
  constructor's at-most-one-bypass grammar. It is a P7-15a reading/design reference, not an immediately
  usable P7-15b validated input or a promise that the production generator can reproduce it. No kernel
  scope change is implied. A production comparison must disclose any map adaptation or map confound.
- **Procedures:** trap warning precedes contact; a lone victim can escape; neither failed lockpicking
  nor a wrong combination removes the other safe-opening methods. The ankle-height cord can be
  stepped over, not crawled under. A crowbar is optional party gear, not specially supplied by Iona.
  Remote dialogue links directly to Run Nell. Unresolved withdrawal leaves Nell awaiting the watch,
  rather than secretly escaping; negotiated settlements and numerical values are unchanged. No combat
  statistics are deferred: this brief explicitly selects a noncombat caper with surrender behavior.
- **Arithmetic:** 120 = 90 + 30; returning 90 plus Iona's reserve top-up restores 120. A party advance
  is reimbursed once, not paid to Nell twice. A full four-digit search at six seconds per attempt is
  16⅔ hours, rounded to “about seventeen.” Fixed custom trap/check values are authored example policy,
  not generated encounter arithmetic or official balance evidence.
- **Presentation:** the canonical text is rendered into a static HTML reading copy with a persistent
  desktop map, room jump links, an enlarge-map link and a narrow-screen opening link. No JavaScript,
  external fonts, analytics, feedback storage, login flow or application UI implementation is added.
  The HTML guide body is checked against its Markdown source; local links, anchors and image loading
  are checked. Desktop and narrow-screen screenshots were inspected by the assistant.

The creative content, map, and presentation differ from both unmerged candidates and the older
pipeline packets. Any later comparison is unmatched. This packet establishes no generation-quality
improvement, reliable duration, combat capability, or phase acceptance. Its roughly 2,000 words may
still be too much; a stopped read is useful feedback, not an incomplete assignment.

## Feedback burden

No separate worksheet. The reading copy invites an opening-plus-one-room smoke test and a chat reply:
**where you stopped, what was confusing, whether you would continue**. Minutes are optional and must
be reported by the reviewer, not inferred. A completed read can additionally answer “would run?”
and name the biggest repair. Human walkthrough and measured editing evidence remain separate from
this first comprehension check.

The [first human smoke-review record](reviews/first-smoke-review.md) preserves Reese's feedback and
original Entry Hall key. Reese found the adventure fairly clear/coherent so far and would continue,
but could not reliably distinguish read-aloud, DM navigation notes and triggered dialogue. Delivery
labels and speech triggers were then clarified; the completed read below includes that revision.

The [completed read-through record](reviews/complete-read-review.md) preserves Reese's subsequent
findings and conditional run decision: would run with several modifications, preferably made by a
model. The exact reviewed packet is preserved locally under the ignored
`generated/p7-15a-reviewed/complete-read-34b83807/` directory. Reward wording, prop markers, reveal
formatting, the archive/payroll explanation and Iona's behavior were corrected afterward.

The [Nell-plan follow-up](reviews/nell-plan-review.md) records further human feedback: the remainder
still seemed fine, but the warning, standoff plan, apparent exits, crowbar supply and a few procedures
remained unclear. Its exact pre-correction packet is preserved under ignored
`generated/p7-15a-reviewed/nell-plan-8690fea9/`. In the subsequent
[near-ready assessment](reviews/near-ready-assessment.md), Reese described the revised guide as
“at least 95% good,” with only minor edits left. That is an assessment of this human-revised reference,
not a measured first-pass success rate, review time or tabletop result. No fresh full read-through
is requested.
